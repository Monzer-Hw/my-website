# Intent: Personal website
Author: Monzer Hweit (AI Engineer). Status: draft.

## Problem
My work currently lives only in GitHub repositories and on LinkedIn.
People who come across me (recruiters, potential clients, fellow
engineers) have no quick way to see what I have built or how I
approach AI engineering problems.

## Proposed outcome
A personal website that presents my projects clearly and makes it
easy to get in touch. The first version is a simple static portfolio.

Goals, in priority order:
1. Showcase my projects
2. Build a personal brand through writing (not in v1, see Constraints)
3. Attract inbound opportunities
4. Act as a single hub for CV, links and contact

v1 is successful when:
- It is live and I am proud to share the link
- Visitors understand my projects without opening GitHub
- I get contacted through the site

## Affected users and systems
- Visitors: a mixed audience of recruiters and hiring managers,
  freelance or consulting clients, and technical peers. No single
  primary audience.
- Existing presence: GitHub (Monzer-Hw), LinkedIn (monzer-hweit)
- Repositories of the projects to be showcased

## Constraints
- v1 scope: simple static portfolio
- Hosting on Vercel; a custom domain will be bought later
- English at launch; German to be added in a later version
- Blog / writing is not considered for now
- Based in Germany: legal page requirements may apply (see Open questions)
