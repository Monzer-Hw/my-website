# Intent: Personal website
Author: Monzer Hweit (AI Engineer). Status: draft.

## Problem
My work is spread across GitHub and LinkedIn. People in the industry
who come across me have no single place to quickly see who I am, what
I have built, and how to reach me.

## Proposed outcome
A professional personal website that shows who I am, my projects, my
experience and my tech stack, and makes it easy to contact me. Each
project is shown with a demo video, a link to try it, and a link to
its code on GitHub where available.

It has worked when:
- The site is live and I am proud to share the link
- A visitor understands what I build without opening GitHub
- People contact me through the site

## Affected users and systems
- Visitors: anyone in the industry (companies, recruiters, clients,
  fellow engineers)
- My existing profiles: GitHub (Monzer-Hw), LinkedIn (monzer-hweit)
- The projects I showcase: their repositories and demo videos

## Constraints
- First version only. A blog, a German version and an AI assistant
  are separate, later intents.
- Hosted on Vercel, with my own domain (not bought yet)
- English only for now
- I live in Germany, so German legal rules for websites apply
  (Impressum and privacy policy)

## Open questions
- Which projects do I show first?
- How should visitors contact me: email, LinkedIn, or a form?
- Should my CV be downloadable?
- Where are the demo videos hosted?
- What visual style should the site have?
